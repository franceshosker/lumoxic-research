# Lumoxic Research

Research from the [Lumoxic AI](https://lumoxicai.me) team on model compression and energy-efficient AI.

## Publications

### 2026

1. **Unified Post-Training Quantization for Heterogeneous Architectures**
   *Lumoxic AI Research* -- Preprint
   > INT8/INT4 dynamic quantization across CNN, Transformer, and hybrid models via single API. 4-8x compression, <0.5% accuracy loss.

2. **Energy-Aware Model Optimization: Measuring Carbon Cost of Inference**
   *Lumoxic AI Research* -- In Review
   > Per-inference Joule measurement for carbon-aware deployment decisions.

### 2025

3. **Structured Pruning Without Sparse Matrix Support**
   *Lumoxic AI Research* -- Published
   > Channel and attention-head pruning producing dense models for standard hardware.
